# Feature: card-actions (card button layout like the reference + quantity stepper on the card)

Redesign the catalog card actions following the user's reference screenshot, and let users change/remove quantities directly from the card without opening the cart or the product detail. Design first with gentle-design, then implement the chosen artboard.

## Specs

- **S1 — Button layout like the reference, without favorite.** L1: "me gustaria que los botones queden asi distribuidos sin el favorito y que en vez de descargar pdf diga la ficha tecnica". Reference (`design/card-actions/reference.png`): horizontal card (photo left; name, "Código: …", price right); below, a row of secondary outlined buttons ([♡] [PDF Descargar PDF] [Ver detalle]); below that a full-width primary "Agregar al carrito" button. For SICOR: no favorite button; the PDF button reads "Ficha técnica" (only when the product has one); keep "Ver detalle" (opens the product sheet); compact primary "Agregar" button, natural width or capped at 140–160px (L4 supersedes the full-width proposal); horizontal photo + information followed by one compact action row or at most two tight rows, with modest visible surfaces and ≥44px hit targets; no price — "Consultar cotización" stays (quote-only). Keep SICOR tokens (design/system.md), not the reference's blue.
- **S2 — Quantity stepper on the card.** L1: "si quiero elimina producto del carrito tengo que entrar al carrito y eliminar estaria bueno que sea - 2 + y si esta en 0 lo elimina y no hace falta entre detalle". Once a product is in the quote cart, the card's add button becomes a stepper "− N +"; "−" at 1 removes the item from the cart (back to the add button); changes persist in the cart store and update the cart count; no need to open the cart or the detail. Only "− N +" is visible in the stepper, with a restrained neutral border: no "en tu cotización", check marks, persistent added messages or instructional card copy. Products with talle/color variants show compact "Opciones" (opens the sheet to choose) or "Editar · N" (opens the sheet to manage variant lines). L4 supersedes the earlier verbose, full-width design treatment; cart-store persistence remains a T2 implementation requirement.
- **S3 — gentle-design first.** L1: "hace de nuevo gentle desing para aplicar estas mejoras". Produce artboards under `design/card-actions/`, user picks, then implement in the real stack. The current overall catalog design stays (catalog-redesign L10: "Ahora como está el producto, está perfecto").
